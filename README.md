# Hark Opal — concept site

A Hark Digital concept direction: **a light gallery after dark** — neon,
grown up. Frost's world (a black studio, thick frosted and polished glass,
razor bevels, hairline light) lit by Neon's material (glass tubes of coloured
gas), but the colour is always seen THROUGH glass: diffused into soft opal
gradients, and crisp only where a design is polished clear through the frost.
Dan Flavin behind sandblasted glass; a James Turrell colour field. The chapter
cut is a colour field: the frame dissolves into its own light, then the next
room resolves out of it.

Built from `Hark Concept Starter` (see `../HARK-CONCEPT-PLAYBOOK.md`), with
the glass kit from `Hark Frost` and the tube kit from `Hark Neon`.

**Live:** https://harkdigital.github.io/hark-opal/

| Chapter | Room | Scene |
|---|---|---|
| hero | Threshold | the Hark mark polished clear through a monumental lit frosted slab |
| work | Light Boxes | six works as museum light boxes; the nine others etched into a lit wall panel |
| services | Spectrum | eleven frosted fins, one spectrum of light, eleven etched service icons |
| voices | Afterglow | a Turrell aperture — one colour field per quote |
| shield | Night Watch | alarm light seeping through frost, cooling to ice as the glass clears |
| process | The Studio | four glass sheets from sketch to lit piece; the stats polished into a lit bar |
| contact | Foyer | "Say hello." polished into a long light box; the gallery dims to it |

Kit: `src/kit/opal.ts` (dusk palette, light cards, tube banks, etch maps that
polish designs clear, `opalBox`, `Dimmer`, stone floor), `src/kit/glass.ts`
(from Frost), `src/kit/neon.ts` (from Neon), `src/kit/pace.ts` (`StoryClock`:
time-paced montages, flash-safe).
